## 🎮 About the Game

Deck & Conn is a single-player turn-based tactics and strategy simulation developed by Funtime Electrics and published by MicroProse Software. It was released on September 1, 2026. Set fifteen minutes into the future of 1989, the game places you in command of a nuclear-powered space corvette during a Cold War-inspired interstellar conflict.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Deck & Conn**. This trainer provides optional assistance with Deck & Conn's reactor power, ship systems, crew management, patrol resources, and tactical encounters. It is designed to reduce resource pressure while preserving the game's command-focused structure, including turn-based combat, system management, ship upgrades, crew promotions, and capture-or-destroy decisions.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore Deck & Conn's campaign, ship systems, crew progression, patrol structure, and tactical combat with fewer resource constraints.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Deck & Conn uses turn-based command navigation across sectors and quadrants rather than real-time space flight. Plan movement carefully, monitor available power, and use docking, station access, and faster-than-light travel when appropriate. Trainer movement assistance is intended to reduce the cost of navigation without replacing the game's sector-based decision-making.

### Tools & Inventory

There is no conventional character inventory system. Progression centers on ship systems, reactor power, patrol resources, upgrades, and crew assignments. Use resource options before repairs, resupply, or upgrades, and turn them off when checking campaign progression or completing scripted objectives.

### World & Environment

The campaign follows dynamic war patrols shaped by HQ orders, distress calls, unidentified contacts, ship upgrades, crew promotions, and the consequences of earlier decisions. Standalone combat scenarios provide focused encounters outside the main campaign. Save before using major trainer options during patrol transitions or promotion events.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is turn-based and depends on positioning, targeting, system management, crew performance, and limited resources. The trainer can help preserve power, stabilize ship systems, and weaken hostile vessels, allowing players to study engagement flow and choose whether to capture or destroy enemy ships without exhausting the corvette's reserves.
